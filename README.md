### 📌 About SujetBacDz
[SujetBacDz.com](https://sujetbacdz.com) is Algeria's dedicated educational web platform designed to assist high school students in preparing for the **National Baccalaureate Examination (شهادة البكالوريا)** and guiding new graduates through **University Orientation (التوجيه الجامعي)**.

* 📚 **2,100+ Official Past Papers & Ministerial Corrections (2008–2026)** across all 6 national streams.
* 🧮 **Interactive BAC Grade Calculator** updated to ministerial Decree 186.
* 🎓 **University Orientation Database** covering 36 majors, higher national schools (ENSCS, ENSIA, ESI, EPAU), medicine, paramedical, and verified admission averages.
* 💡 **Methodology & Exam Guides** for Natural Sciences, Physics, Philosophy, and French written by official inspectors.
